We propose a native Go SDK contribution aligned with TxFlow engineering.
A self-funded offline foundation is available; live contracts, permission,
signing and real streaming remain to be built. No official affiliation is claimed.

Preferred request: **$27,500 / estimated 220h**, subject to scope agreement.

| 5: hardening | Confirmed limits/errors, race/stress/leak/benchmarks and hooks | $5,000 |
| 6: ecosystem release | Reviewed release, docs/starter examples, version policy and stabilization | $3,750 |

additional bot/market-maker tooling and maintenance. Effort is approximately
nine working weeks for Full after access and timely reviews; protocol alignment
can change that horizon. Unsupported features should be re-scoped explicitly.
Payment follows accepted deliverables, not undocumented implementation attempts.
